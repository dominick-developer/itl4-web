1. Introduction and Our Privacy Commitment

TANZATECH SOLUTIONS ("we", "us", "our") operates IT Level Four Connect and IT Expert Academy. We are committed to protecting your personal data in accordance with the Personal Data Protection Act, 2022 (Tanzania) and its regulations. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, why, how we protect it, and your rights.

3. Account and Registration Information

When you register, we collect your full name, admission number (used as username), phone number, email address, gender (optional), role (Student/CR), and a password (stored as a secure hash — never in plain text). Email verification codes are stored temporarily (15 minutes).

4. Academic and Learning Information

We collect your course enrolments, lesson completions, quiz attempts, scores, and certificates. This data is used to track progress, issue certificates, and improve educational content.

5. Attendance, Assignments, and Academic Records

Class attendance, assignment submissions, grades, and feedback are stored. These records are accessible to you, your CR (for attendance), and your Lecturers (for assessment).

6. User-Generated Content

Assignments, projects, portfolio items, and uploaded files are stored. You can control the visibility of portfolio items (private or public). Public items are visible at /academy/p/{user_id} without authentication. Never upload passwords, API keys, or sensitive personal information.

  • What is stored locally: Full conversation history, token usage, and estimated cost.

8. Coding Environment Data

Code you write in the Coding Lab is stored in your private project workspace. Execution of Python code occurs entirely in your browser via WebAssembly (Pyodide) — no code is sent to our servers for execution. HTML/CSS/JavaScript previews run in isolated sandboxed iframes. Execution logs (metadata only) may be recorded for security and analytics.

17. Your Privacy Rights under PDPA 2022

Under the Personal Data Protection Act, 2022, you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of your personal data;
  • Correction — request correction of inaccurate data;
  • Deletion — request deletion of your data (subject to legal retention obligations);
  • Objection — object to certain processing;
  • Portability — request export of your data in a machine-readable format;
  • Withdraw consent — where processing is based on consent;
  • Complaint — lodge a complaint with the Personal Data Protection Commission of Tanzania.

18. Access, Correction, and Deletion Procedures

To exercise your rights, email privacy@smartbizsenseapp.com OWNER CONFIRMATION with:

  • Your full name and admission number;
  • The right you wish to exercise;
  • Reasonable verification of your identity.

We will respond within 30 days as required by PDPA 2022. Requests that would impair another person's privacy or violate legal retention obligations may be limited.

19. Cookies, Sessions, and Similar Technologies

We use:

20. Privacy of Minors

The Platform is intended for users aged 13 and above. Users under 18 must have parental or guardian consent. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has registered, contact us for immediate deletion.

21. Data Breach and Incident Response

If we become aware of a personal data breach that poses a risk to your rights, we will:

  • Notify affected users without undue delay;
  • Notify the Personal Data Protection Commission of Tanzania where required by law;
  • Take immediate steps to contain and remediate the breach;
  • Document the incident for audit purposes.
